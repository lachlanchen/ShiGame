## Design intent

Promises must be judged by actual field results, not merely the selected plan. A costly evacuation cannot automatically certify that everyone arrived. Characters wait for answers, leave unconfirmed names open, and distinguish silence from confirmed death. These personal reactions replace generic outcome summaries in the reaction and chronicle; the same resolved promise is used in the ending.

The player can change local outcomes through three saved field commands. These changes do not rewrite established historical events. Troop movement, risk, resources and subsequent arrival remain deterministic and replayable.
