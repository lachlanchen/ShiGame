# Production playability standard

“Playable” in SHI means a player can form an intention, act on it, understand the response, recover or change method, and reach a coherent authored outcome. A beautiful screen, a clickable story, a systems spreadsheet and a cinematic are each insufficient on their own.

## Public-alpha acceptance

Chapter I must provide, and every later chapter must preserve:

1. **A readable situation.** The player can see the current act, scene, date, site, field condition, resources, commitment, enemy posture and uncertainty before committing.
2. **A deliberate order.** Selection is reversible. One complete focused reading exposes immediate effects, strategic method, obligations, known counterplay and uncertainty. Only an explicit issue-order action commits.
3. **A consequential answer.** Action, commitment, authored pressure, enemy pursuit, method read and field condition resolve as separate visible layers and are recorded.
4. **A campaign horizon.** Time and place progress authorially; routes may converge but cannot silently move backward, skip an act or erase an obligation.
5. **Counterplay.** Qin observes pressure and repeated methods. The player can reduce exposure, change method, accept a cost or deliberately take a risk.
6. **Army and polity pressure.** Grain, trust, momentum, people and exposure are operational resources. The longer campaign will add force composition, readiness, officers, territory, administration, diplomacy and intelligence without collapsing them into one power score.
7. **Failure with meaning.** Capture and scattering are possible, disclosed and replayable; recovery turns preserve tension. Failure is not a hidden random punishment.
8. **Different outcomes.** At least three conclusions arise from accumulated state and commitments. Counterfactual freedom changes history inside plausible constraints rather than granting consequence-free fantasy.
9. **Replay value.** A second route must reveal a materially different promise, pressure/countermethod pattern or conclusion—not just alternate dialogue.
10. **Access and control parity.** Pointer, keyboard, touch and controller use one resolver. Required information is never sound-, color- or hover-only. Save/load preserves authoritative history.

## Evidence gate

A feature is not accepted as playable until automated rules, a complete visible browser or engine route, and visual inspection all agree. Native-engine claims require native execution; source compilation or screenshots of an editor are not substitutes. Human first-time-player observation, physical controller, assistive technology, listening and specialist historical review remain separate gates.

## Unreal cinematic priority

Unreal is the feature-first cinematic client after the schema-v7 horizon checkpoint. Its first risky slice is intentionally end-to-end:

```text
inspect the rain field → select an order → review the council consequence
→ issue it → watch people/terrain/opponent answer → advance act/site/time
```

The camera, lighting, blocking, performance, sound and environment must clarify this loop. A noninteractive Sequencer film does not close the gate. The Web client remains the fastest playable reference; Unity remains a maintained shared-schema baseline while Unreal reaches and then exceeds its interaction parity. No client may invent a different campaign truth.
