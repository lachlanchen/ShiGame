# Chapter I research brief: Daze Village

Status: schema-v3 evidence-location pass complete; specialist review incomplete.

## Secure baseline

The received *Shiji* narrative associates Chen Sheng and Wu Guang with a group of conscripts delayed by heavy rain on the way to a northern posting in 209 BCE, followed by an uprising at Daze Village. Later narratives connect this crisis to the broader collapse of Qin and subsequent claimants invoking Chu.

## Deliberately qualified points

- The familiar statement that all late arrivals would be executed is part of the received narrative. Its relationship to excavated Qin law and actual enforcement requires specialist legal-historical review.
- Exact numbers, speeches, omens, private motives, sequence timing, and crowd unanimity are not treated as transparent eyewitness facts.
- The future roles of Xiang Yu and Liu Bang are not knowable to characters at Daze. Chapter I uses only uncertain reports of regions and networks.
- The map is schematic. Coordinates in the campaign JSON serve layout, not geographic measurement.

## Fiction boundary

The Keeper, Aunt Yu, Courier Han, grain tallies, the damaged ford decision, and all private council dialogue are project inventions. Chen Sheng and Wu Guang are historical figures placed within attested broad circumstances, but their exact in-game words are invented.
