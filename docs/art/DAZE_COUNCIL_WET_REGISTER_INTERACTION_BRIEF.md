# Daze council wet-register hand-interaction brief

Status: Gate 5A authored interaction contract · engineering blockout only · not final prop, hand animation, historical reconstruction or close-camera approval

## Why this interaction exists

The first hand-performance slice belongs only to Chapter I node `rain-order`, with Chen Sheng as the canonical speaker. It makes one sentence physically legible:

> “If the register reaches the next post, we are nine hundred isolated offenders. If it does not, what are we?”

The words are SHI-authored dramatization, not a transcript. The received histories support the delayed conscripts, rain, expected missed deadline, Chen Sheng and Wu Guang, and the reported scale of nine hundred; they do not attest this council, this wording, the Keeper, or a surviving register. The interaction therefore stages a political problem without presenting an invented object or gesture as archaeological fact.

The dramatic action is restrained: Chen Sheng already supports the wet register in his left hand, brings his right hand onto its upper edge, holds the nine hundred names between both hands during the conditional question, then releases the right hand after “what are we?” The left hand never lets the register fall. The absence of an answer—not a flourish—completes the beat.

The campaign context begins with the Keeper/player holding the soaked register. Gate 5A therefore opens **after an offscreen pass** to Chen Sheng; it does not depict or imply that the Keeper never held it. A visible Keeper-to-Chen transfer would require two synchronized rigs, an explicit ownership transition and a separate story/camera contract. It is deferred rather than hidden inside this one-character contact proof.

This is the first concrete test of the project rule `ground → offer → regard → consequence`. It does not add dialogue, alter a choice, preselect an order, or make the animation authoritative.

- campaign node is exactly `rain-order`;

It may not borrow another character's hand pose, silently substitute an object, attach the prop to the camera, or leave an invisible collision proxy active.

This gate does **not** authorize:

- final hand anatomy, nails, calluses, wet-skin response, cloth contact, sleeve simulation or close framing;
- automatic lip sync, voice, music, sound effects, facial emotion inference or generated performance;

## Stage and prop blockout

The current command surface ends at world `Z = 14 cm`; it is a map/ground presentation surface, not a reachable council table. The interaction must not fake hand contact with it. The register remains hand-held, centered in the existing `Z = 92–100 cm` council evidence band so the accepted 44-degree camera can read face, hands, object and wet ground in one sentence.

The engineering prop is a texture-free, nonmetallic, non-emissive abstract bound volume. Its provisional envelope is `32 × 14 × 2 cm`, with rounded safe edges and no writing, seal, page block, wood grain, slip grooves or modern book cues. These dimensions describe a blocking target only. They are not a historical claim and may change after named human review.

The prop has:

- one stable origin at its center;
- named left-support, right-contact and camera-readability markers;
- no skeleton, morphs, simulation, collision, overlap events, input or gameplay tags;
- one stage-owned lifetime, never saved or replicated;
- one muted clay material so silhouette and contact errors remain visible;
- provenance that records Blender, generator/export scripts if any, licenses, source hashes and every review decision.

The left-hand socket owns the prop transform throughout this first slice. Right-hand contact is solved against the prop markers; the prop is never reparented during the clip. This prevents a one-frame ownership pop at release.

The accepted council skeletal component currently carries a `100×` world scale while a normally imported static prop already uses Unreal centimeters. Attachment must therefore use an explicit compensated relative scale—provisionally `0.01`—or a verified keep-world-scale route. Runtime admission measures the resulting world envelope and requires the exact `32 × 14 × 2 cm` blockout dimensions; an approximately 100-times oversized or undersized prop fails closed before presentation.

## Four-second performance contract

The source clip is exactly `121` frames at `30 fps`, frames `1–121` inclusive, representing four seconds between the first and last sample. It is non-looping, deterministic and root-stationary. No random offset, audio amplitude, transcript analysis or frame-rate-dependent phase selection is permitted.

| Source frame | Unreal sample | Time | Authored state | Required read |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| `1` | `0` | `0.000 s` | `start` | Left hand supports the lower edge; right hand is open and moving from rest; register and eyeline are already readable. |
| `31` | `30` | `1.000 s` | `bilateral-contact` | Right palm/fingers acquire the upper edge without a wrist snap; both hands visibly share the object. |
| `61` | `60` | `2.000 s` | `held-question` | Both contacts hold. Stillness carries “If it does not”; shoulders and root do not inflate the gesture. |

Interpolation may refine the path between these five authored states, but it cannot add another semantic beat. Finger release proceeds little/ring → middle → index/thumb only if deformation review confirms that the ordering reads naturally; otherwise the named reviewers must replace it with a simpler accepted release rather than preserving an artificial flourish.

The face may use the already admitted silent-intent deformation only as a subordinate layer. It cannot become the sole carrier of the question, and this hand gate cannot promote the generic face or skin candidate to final art.

## Contact, deformation and camera gates

The clip remains an engineering candidate until all automated and watched checks pass:

- exact skeleton name, 53-bone hierarchy and bind pose are unchanged;
- root translation and yaw remain within `0.1 cm` and `0.1°` of the admitted stage transform;
- arm-chain scale stays exactly `1.0`; no IK stretch, NaN, discontinuity or negative scale is admitted;
- left-hand prop drift remains at or below `0.25 cm` and `0.25°` at every sample;
- right contact begins once, remains continuous through the hold, and releases once;
- visible finger/prop penetration does not exceed `0.4 cm`; visible floating contact does not exceed `0.8 cm` at the admitted medium camera;
- wrist, elbow and shoulder silhouettes remain finite and anatomically plausible through front, three-quarter and profile engineering views;
- sleeves never hide every contact point or tear through the hands;
- the 44-degree council camera retains the register, both hands, Chen Sheng's regard, affected ground and subtitle-safe negative space;
- no close lens is used to conceal a bad contact or to imply final hand/face quality;
- the terminal frame remains stable for at least one second without restarting the clip.

Metrics are rejection aids, not substitutes for animation judgment. A technically bounded pose still fails if it reads as presenting a sacred object, surrendering a book, performing magic, pleading theatrically, or announcing a foreknown imperial destiny.

## Reduced-motion and accessibility behavior

Reduced motion preserves the same five pieces of information as held poses/cuts: start, acquired contact, shared hold, ordered release and settled consequence. It suppresses continuous reach interpolation and never repeats. The prop cannot teleport between hands, so each cut must preserve ownership and screen direction. Skipping the beat goes directly to the exact terminal state and cannot preselect or issue a choice.

The object and contact must remain distinguishable without relying on skin tone, red/green coding, specular sparkle, motion blur or audio. Subtitles and the historical/dramatization disclosure remain readable at 200% text and in RTL layouts. Keyboard, controller and pointer paths may pause, skip or replay the presentation only through one shared semantic command; none may change campaign truth differently.

## Historical and cultural review boundary

Before the abstract volume can become a production prop, named reviewers must answer:

- What kind of record could plausibly make this group legible to a Qin administrative chain, and what remains unknown?
- Does the chosen object form accidentally resemble a later codex, modern ledger, court document, rank tablet or ritual object?
- Are material, dimensions, bindings, handling, wet response and writing direction supportable, or clearly labeled reconstruction?
- Does Chen Sheng's handling read as practical responsibility under rain and coercion rather than reverence, illiteracy, villainy or revolutionary destiny?
- Can the Keeper's relationship to the register remain meaningful without inventing an elite scholar identity?
- Do Chinese historical-material, cultural-performance, hand-animation/anatomy, cinematic, accessibility and localization reviewers independently accept the same in-engine shots?

Until those questions close, public wording is `PROJECT-ORIGINAL WET-REGISTER INTERACTION BLOCKOUT · DRAMATIC RECONSTRUCTION · NOT A SURVIVING QIN REGISTER`.

## Tool and evidence plan

Blender `4.5.12 LTS` is the appropriate editable source tool for this geometry-free/texture-free interaction and the existing 53-bone rig. A deterministic validator should sample all 121 frames, bind the exact bones and contact markers, and produce source, interchange and render receipts. Unreal admission must be inspect-only by default, use an explicit mutation gate, preserve the accepted character/facial/skin assets, and emit exact runtime markers for route admission, bilateral contact, ordered release, terminal clamp and unchanged campaign/save state.

The package proof requires a fresh cook, normal and reduced-motion watched runs on the single retained noVNC stack, captured contact/hold/release/terminal frames, clean material/animation/fallback scans, and byte-stable campaign-save evidence. Human reviewers then decide whether the beat communicates the story; automated success alone cannot promote it.

Image generation, AgInTiFlow, Musia and Xiaoyunque are intentionally not used for this first silent texture-free hand proof. They become candidates only when a named bottleneck exists: reviewed prop surface reference, rights-cleared sound/music, or shot-locked previs. Availability is not a reason to generate content, and no paid generation is submitted without explicit user confirmation.
