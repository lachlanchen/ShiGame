# SHI Daze field-shelter brief

Status: production blockout contract · open rain shelter for the Daze command position · fictional practical construction, not an excavated or attested late-Qin building.

## Production problem

The accepted command ground and wet field now establish material contact, weather pressure and playable scale, but the council still floats under an empty sky. The next bounded increment must shelter the human decision plane without becoming palace shorthand, a decorative pavilion or a wall around the player.

This asset closes shelter architecture only. It does not approve final rain, vegetation, characters, formations, sky, lighting or historical reconstruction.

## Narrative and spatial purpose

The shelter must make three relations legible:

1. the command position is temporary and exposed to weather;
2. the council protects a small working surface, not status or ceremony;
3. the wet road and field remain visible beyond an open structure.

The exact frame, spans, pitch, lashings and mat construction are project-authored dramatic environment design. Broad material choices—wood, plant-fibre matting and cord—are plausible production vocabulary, not proof of a specific Daze structure.

## Geometry contract

- Identity-root static mesh, centered on the command space.
- Four primary round worked-wood posts at approximately `X = ±3.6 m`, `Y = ±2.6 m`, outside the `5.8 × 3.7 m` command-ground footprint and all accepted site/signal clearance.
- An open `8.4 × 6.6 m` roof envelope with eaves no lower than `2.78 m`, a roof surface near `3.37 m` and no structural point above `3.45 m`.
- A shallow two-plane pitch sheds rain without reading as a house, temple, tent fantasy or tiled pavilion.
- Longitudinal/cross beams and sparse braces explain load paths. Restrained cord bands mark connections without claiming an exact archaeological knot or relying on small ornamental detail.
- Overlapping woven-reed roof panels remain thick enough to preserve silhouette and avoid one-sided disappearance. Coarse relief must read at field scale; no dense micro-weave geometry.
- No wall, door, raised floor, throne zone, banner pole, finial, bracket set, tiled eave, written mark or decorative carving.
- LOD0 target below 12,000 triangles; LOD1 below 4,000. UV0 and non-overlapping lightmap UV1 on every engine LOD. Nanite remains off unless measured scene needs change that decision.
- No source or runtime collision. The shelter is a non-authoritative presentation layer and may not intercept pointer rays, movement, navigation or engagement state.

## Material contract

### Rain-darkened wood

- Nonmetallic, broadly rough dark wood with low-frequency longitudinal variation.
- No varnish, glossy furniture finish, orange cedar or carved palace language.
- No normal input until an authored texture set survives scale, tiling and relighting review.

### Woven reed mat

- Desaturated straw/olive-brown darkened by rain; never bright yellow thatch.
- Broad alternating panel/strand direction may be expressed through vertex color and low-frequency material variation, not a noisy procedural checker.
- Opaque, two-sided only if engine review proves underside visibility requires it; no translucent plastic-sheet appearance.

### Coarse cord

- Near-black brown fibre, nonmetallic and rough.
- Bands explain ties at reading distance but cannot become jewelry, decorative knots or unsupported close-up archaeology.

## Camera and runtime contract

- The accepted 44° council lens must retain speaker, Keeper-side negative space, command weight and decision plane without a post crossing the face/object relation.
- The command-surface, wet-field and Broken Crossing review cameras must retain their full intended subjects.
- The shelter remains visible during campaign and non-authoritative engagement, including all three pulses and the return to council.
- Exact asset path, identity transform, bounds, three material slots, LODs, UVs, collision-free state, noninteraction and camera-clearance rules fail closed before runtime spawn.
- A development-only shelter camera may inspect roof, posts and platform clearance. It is excluded from Shipping behavior and cannot replace the dialogue lens.

## Rejection list

Reject any pass that reads as a tiled imperial pavilion, later-period architectural stereotype, fantasy tent, ceremonial canopy, modern tarpaulin, bright straw hut, solid room, toy kit, floating roof, impossible beam load, post through the council/command surface, dense geometry noise, baked lighting, fabricated writing, collision authority or historical reconstruction claim.

## Acceptance path

1. deterministic Blender source plus oblique, underside and profile inspection;
2. clean GLB/FBX round trip with topology, UV, material, bounds, LOD and camera-clearance receipts;
3. isolated Unreal import and authored-material inspection with read-only hash preservation;
4. native positive and hostile presentation tests;
5. clean Linux package and headless smoke proving the exact asset delta;
6. visible shelter-review, opening council, story progression, complete Broken Crossing and unchanged campaign return;
7. physical-display performance repeated after rain, vegetation and figures/formations;
8. human historical-material, environment, accessibility and cinematic review before final status.

Approval is deliberately narrow: a usable packaged shelter production blockout, not final cinematic environment or an attested reconstruction.

## 2026-08-10 bounded acceptance

The accepted LOD0/LOD1 sources contain `3,948 / 460` triangles, two UV channels per LOD, three material slots and zero collision. Unreal 5.8.1 preserves exact identity-root bounds of approximately `8.40 × 6.73 × 3.55 m`, authors compile-clean rain-darkened wood, woven-reed and coarse-cord graphs, and fail-closes scale, collision, navigation, disclosure, final-art, post-clearance, eave and height drift. The clean Linux package increases the prior `505`-package boundary by exactly four assets to `509`; a clean smoke mounts all `509` and loads `ShiGameMode`.

The visible localhost review then advanced Act I → Act III through two committed orders, completed all three Broken Crossing pulses with **Orderly crossing**, and returned to the same `41 / 68 / 17 / 80 / 63` campaign resources, turn and decision count. The shelter persisted in council, consequence and engagement views without owning input, collision, navigation or campaign authority. Earlier checker-color, beam/rafter roof penetration, lashing-axis and LOD-silhouette passes were rejected and corrected before this acceptance.
