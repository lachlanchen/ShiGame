# SHI Daze council character blockout brief

Status: skeletal production-blockout contract · five canonical Chapter I identities · not final anatomy, facial performance or historical costume reconstruction.

## Production problem

The accepted Unreal command space still represents the Keeper and every named speaker with the same cylinder, sphere and cube assembly. Color and text preserve canonical identity, but the scene cannot yet communicate posture, labor, social relation, rain-heavy clothing or hand scale. This gate replaces the anonymous primitives with five reproducible skeletal silhouettes while preserving the existing two-slot council transaction, dialogue, provenance, click target, stencil and 44-degree camera contract.

This is a production blockout, not a beauty-character pass. It closes body scale, silhouette identity, bone hierarchy, broad garment layering, hand availability and clean engine interchange. It does not authorize close facial framing, final costume claims, voice, lip sync, hair simulation, cloth simulation or promotional use.

## Historical boundary

- The canonical campaign and cited primary histories establish the late-Qin Daze setting, conscript crisis and named historical participants. They do not specify the exact clothing worn by these five people in the dramatized council.
- The China National Museum overview for Wang Fang's *The Formation of Hanfu: Archaeological Research on Clothing from the Eastern Zhou, Qin and Han* describes a diverse Qin-to-early-Han clothing field and treats upper clothing, lower clothing, headwear/hair, material, pattern and color as separate archaeological questions: <https://www.chnmuseum.cn/yj/xscg/xszz/202408/t20240815_269916.shtml>.
- The blockout may therefore use only non-specific practical layers: wrapped/crossing upper volume, belted waist, lower work layer, tied sleeves, simple footwear volume, plain hair mass and a role-readable carried shape. These are form-language tests, not claims that a named cut, fastening, textile, dye, rank marker or hairstyle is attested for Daze in 209 BCE.
- Every identifiable garment construction, headwear, hair arrangement, shoe, armor element, seal container and satchel remains an item-level historical red gate. No garment may be called `shenyi`, `ru`, `pao`, official uniform or reconstructed Qin clothing merely because its silhouette appears plausible.
- No crown, imperial dragon, ornate lamellar, later stirrup/cavalry language, fantasy shoulder armor, court robe, modern seam/pocket, modern casual clothing, jewelry shorthand or clean ceremonial silk.
- Runtime disclosure: `SKELETAL COUNCIL CHARACTER PRODUCTION BLOCKOUT · GENERIC PRACTICAL LAYERS · NOT AN EXACT 209 BCE COSTUME OR PORTRAIT RECONSTRUCTION`.

## Authoring and rights contract

- Blender `4.5.12 LTS` is installed alongside the existing environment-authoring Blender rather than replacing it. MPFB `2.0.17` at commit `80919fa4682335c41847f761a4d79dcad4124732` supplies only a CC0 basemesh/weight baseline and its built-in 53-bone `game_engine` rig.
- MPFB program code remains GPL-3.0-or-later and is an offline authoring dependency, not runtime or repository-vendored code. The bundled basemesh/rig data and official MakeHuman system asset pack are CC0. Generated character output is recorded as editable project data under the upstream output policy.
- Do not use MPFB's bundled modern casual clothing or hair as SHI costume. Do not use community assets, remote MakeHuman service, online asset downloader, neural character generator, hidden model weights or private portrait input.
- SHI-authored blockout garments, hair masses, satchels, materials, poses, exports, renders and validation remain project source. Imported CC0 contribution and project-authored contribution must stay explicit in provenance.
- The first technical trial proved a clean 53-bone FBX with full three-segment finger chains, armature binding, `26,756` body triangles and `1.051 × 0.430 × 1.659 m` neutral bounds. It also proved that the upstream sample's texture paths are not portable. Production exports must therefore carry no texture dependency; Unreal authors the bounded blockout materials.

## Skeletal and geometry contract

- One shared skeleton name and exact bone list across all five exports. Minimum bones: root/pelvis/spine, clavicle, upper/lower arm, hand, all five three-segment finger chains, neck/head, thigh/calf/foot/ball on both sides.
- Body deformation must remain bound to the skeleton after a clean FBX round trip. No leaf bones, animation bake, control rig, shape-key promise or texture path in this milestone.
- Real meter scale in Blender and centimeter scale in Unreal. Neutral character height must remain inside `155–183 cm`; identity-root transform and armature scale must be positive and applied.
- Hands, wrists, elbows, shoulders, knees and neck stay unobscured enough for deformation review. Garment blockout may be coarse, but it cannot fuse the arms to the torso, seal both legs into an unusable tube or cover every finger.
- Each asset uses named separable meshes for body, upper layer, lower layer, sleeves/leg layers, hair mass and role prop where present. Mesh names and material slots are deterministic.
- Neutral blockout budget per character: no more than `55k` render triangles across body and authored layers, no more than eight material slots and one 53-bone skeleton. LOD generation, facial rig and cinematic groom are separate gates, not fabricated here.
- Collision remains the existing bounded council click capsule/actor contract. Character render meshes do not generate per-poly gameplay collision, overlaps or navigation.

## Material and visual contract

- Texture-free Unreal blockout materials only: shared restrained skin clay, dark hair clay and muted earth/charcoal/umber cloth families. Rough, nonmetallic and relightable; no baked highlights, neural texture, modern textile print, exposed UV checker or emissive identity.
- Color may support identity but must remain secondary to outline, layer length, shoulder shape, prop and stance.
- Neutral-gray front/back/oblique review renders are mandatory so attractive palette cannot hide intersecting garments, collapsed anatomy or indistinguishable silhouettes.
- Wide council framing remains mandatory. Close face shots stay prohibited because this rig has no admitted eyelid, corneal, lip, cheek or speech system.

## Runtime fail-closed contract

Unreal may replace a primitive figure only after exact soft object paths, shared skeleton, participant-to-mesh mapping, material count, height/bounds, identity transform, collision policy, disclosure and final-art false state pass. A missing or drifting skeletal asset must leave the current known-good primitive visible and block false production-character attestation; it may not spawn an invisible participant or silently substitute another identity.

Character rendering remains presentation-only. It cannot tick gameplay authority, receive input directly, replicate, serialize, alter campaign/engagement state, change canonical speaker ordering or weaken save-byte guards. The existing `AShiCouncilFigure` actor remains the click/stencil/participant owner.

## Acceptance record — 2026-08-10

The first packaged pass was rejected twice before acceptance. An inherited Unreal `DefaultPawn` produced a large pale sphere in the character frame, so SHI now explicitly suppresses that pawn and retains its direct command-space camera. The next keeper-lighting passes still produced black face and hand patches. An in-engine unlit diagnostic proved that geometry and material assignment were intact; preserving the authored FBX normals with `FBXNIM_IMPORT_NORMALS` removed the patches without emissive, texture or unlit-material concealment.

The accepted v6 package contains five skeletal meshes, one shared 53-bone skeleton and fourteen texture-free three-node default-lit materials. All five identities were reviewed in the packaged 44-degree council cameras. A fresh normal run selected `read-the-names`, issued it, entered/skipped the prepared cinema, wrote one verified autosaved transaction and advanced from `rain-order` to Aunt Yu's `open-council`. All 18 native SHI suites pass, the Linux package mounts 536 packages, and headless smoke loads `/Engine/Maps/Entry` with `ShiGameMode` before a clean requested exit.

Decision: `approved-runtime-five-identity-skeletal-production-blockout-story-reviewed-not-final-character-art`.

The final-art red gates remain unchanged: neutral reference pose is not animation; generic faces are not portraits; practical layers are not accepted costume reconstruction; close facial cinema, cloth/hair response, interaction hands, authored LODs, physical-display performance and human historical/character/cultural/accessibility/cinematic review remain open.
