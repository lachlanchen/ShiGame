## First subject and approval order

Start with `chen-sheng`, using `keeper` only as the second identity after the
first identity works in more than one view. Do not commission five unrelated
beautiful faces and attempt to reconcile them afterward. Preserve all five
canonical IDs: `keeper`, `chen-sheng`, `wu-guang`, `yu-mu`, `qin-courier`.

Chen's acting intent is practical attention under strain: watching the other
person before speaking, a breath held and released, readiness without a heroic
pose. He is neither an emperor in waiting nor a villain. The Keeper is an
attentive participant carrying a decision, not a chosen savior. Exact age,
facial proportions, hairstyle and dress remain casting choices awaiting review.

Retain the existing silhouette jobs: Chen's longer weather layer; Wu Guang's
shorter asymmetric wrap; the Keeper's clear sleeves/document satchel; Aunt Yu's
planted logistical presence; the courier's narrow, exit-ready silhouette. These
are art-direction continuity, not authenticated Qin garment constructions.

## Required identity packet

- One canonical neutral head image, plus independently checked three-quarter
  and profile views and a full-body front/back pair. Do not accept a contact
  sheet whose panels depict different people.
- Calm, listening, restrained concern and interrupted-speech studies. Expression
  must not change skull, apparent age, eye spacing or hairline.
- Neutral daylight, warm shelter and cold rain studies. Lighting may change;
  complexion, anatomy and identity must remain coherent.
- Written immutable traits versus stateful details: bone structure and hairline
  are identity; wetness, dust, tears and garment damage belong to scene state.
- Separate face, costume, prop and environment references with explicit roles.
  No borrowed celebrity face, actor voice or LALACHAN character reference.
- Exact model/checkpoint/license, original prompt and inputs, seed/settings,
  SHA-256 for each selected image, reviewer, decision and rejection reasons.
- Versions never silently replace one another. Aging or wardrobe changes require
  an explicit approved variant and the scene/year where it first applies.

## Acceptance test before video

1. Show unlabeled neutral and relit views: observers should recognize the same
   character; record the actual responses rather than inventing an approval.
2. Compare paired characters side by side and in a shared medium shot. Reject
   face blending, twins, body-scale changes or color-only identity.
3. Inspect anatomy, hands, teeth/eyes where visible, cloth intersections and
   costume anachronisms. A better face does not approve a costume or close-up.
4. Bind every shot to the approved identity version and image hashes. Reference
   conditioning is a control, not proof; inspect the generated footage again.
5. Keep the source identity when using a prior shot's last frame. Reject drift
   rather than gradually canonizing the generator's changing face.

Use images as reference for editable final 3D characters where gameplay needs
continuous camera and action. Generated videos cannot supply a reliable combat
rig, interaction collision or deterministic object custody by themselves.
