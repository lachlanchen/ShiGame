# SHI command surface brief

Status: packaged runtime production blockout accepted for council, story and Broken Crossing presentation · original fictional interface-stage architecture · not a late-Qin artifact or reconstruction · final environment, historical-material and human cinematic review open.

## Production problem

Before this checkpoint, the Unreal command space used a `580 × 370 × 16 cm` engine cube with the default white BasicShape material. It preserved tested marker positions and the `Z = 14 cm` interaction plane, but read as an unshaded game-board slab, overwhelmed the command weight, and conflicted with SHI's wet-earth, worked-wood and oxidized-bronze visual thesis.

This pass must replace that visible defect without disguising an unsupported giant table as historical furniture. The replacement is a low **command-ground platform**: an explicitly fictional staging abstraction beneath the field shelter, where physical signals are arranged close to the earth. It is not an attested Qin command practice, excavated object, sand table, measured map or literal reconstruction of Daze Village.

## Player and cinematic purpose

The surface must make the player feel that command is material:

1. people, grain, pursuit and promises have separate physical weights;
2. the command weight touches the same ground as every other signal;
3. the speaker remains a person across the surface, not a miniature to be manipulated;
4. the platform recedes beneath information instead of becoming decorative spectacle;
5. opening council and Broken Crossing retain one stable spatial grammar.

The accepted 44° council frame, site/signal pointer spacing, `Z = 14 cm` contact plane, non-authoritative engagement boundary, consequence cameras and Slate readability may not drift merely to make the asset attractive.

## Form contract

- Exact outer bounds: `580 × 370 × 16 cm`, centered at world origin, from `Z = -2 cm` to `Z = 14 cm`.
- A flat central packed-earth field extends to at least `X = ±285 cm` and `Y = ±180 cm`; every accepted marker and the command weight therefore contacts one continuous plane.
- A restrained perimeter no wider than `5 cm` reads as dark worked wood supporting or containing the field, not as ornamental furniture.
- Outer corners and vertical edges are softened enough to catch light without industrial-perfect or fantasy-carved profiles.
- The top remains flat. Material variation may not falsify collision, make markers float or imply a height field that gameplay does not use.
- Root transform is identity at world origin. Runtime collision and navigation influence are disabled; the surface is never an interaction target or gameplay authority.
- The surface remains visible during the non-authoritative Broken Crossing exercise because its six signals share the same contact grammar.

## Material contract

### Wet packed earth

- Dark clay-brown rather than gray concrete, orange desert sand or glossy mud.
- Broad tamped variation with restrained damp response; no high-frequency procedural noise, tessellated relief, footprints, writing or baked lighting.
- Opaque, one-sided PBR surface with authored base color, roughness, specular and ambient occlusion. Metallic remains zero.
- No normal input is claimed until a reviewed texture/scan or deliberate authored normal source exists.

### Dark worked wood

- Near-black brown with quiet directional grain in color/roughness only.
- Hand-worked support material, not lacquered palace furniture, modern plywood, parquet, precious hardwood display or fantasy carving.
- Opaque, one-sided PBR surface; nonmetallic, highly rough, no unsupported normal input.

Material graphs must be isolated, inspectable, compile-clean and read-only by default. Generated pixels are not required for this pass; deterministic geometry and engine-authored shader graphs are preferable to an unreviewed texture synthesis shortcut.

## Technical delivery

- Editable Blender `.blend` plus GLB and FBX LOD0/LOD1 interchange.
- One Unreal static mesh named `SM_SHI_CommandSurface_01` with two exact slots: `M_SHI_WetPackedEarth` and `M_SHI_DarkWorkedWood`.
- UV0 plus non-overlapping lightmap UV1 on both LODs; explicit centimeter-scale import and recorded bounds.
- LOD0 under 2,000 triangles; LOD1 materially lower. Nanite stays off unless measured need overturns that decision.
- One simple convex outer hull may exist in the source receipt, but runtime explicitly disables collision so it cannot intercept site/signal pointer rays.
- Exact Unreal material author/inspector, source validator, import inspector, hashes and package contents are provenance-bound.
- Native automation rejects wrong mesh identity, scale, bounds, contact plane, interaction/collision authority, hidden engagement state, insufficient marker margin and incompatible command-weight footprint.

## Historical and visual rejection list

Reject:

- paper map, compass rose, grid, written labels, pseudo-Chinese glyphs or decorative calligraphy;
- miniature soldiers, toy buildings, game-board lanes or colored territory paint;
- polished stone floor, palace lacquer, gold trim, modern plywood, metal fasteners or machine-perfect joinery;
- a literal claim that Qin commanders used this platform, or wording that turns project fiction into evidence;
- noisy generated pores, fake cracks at prop scale, puddles that behave like mirrors, baked highlights or an unsupported normal-map effect;
- raised rims that occlude signals, steal pointer hits or make the command weight float;
- a bright field that competes with Slate, faces or bronze signals;
- accepting an isolated beauty render when the packaged council and engagement frames fail.

## Acceptance views and gates

The asset may advance only if all of the following agree:

1. clean Blender front, oblique and underside/profile inspection;
2. clean GLB/FBX validation with exact bounds, components, slots, UVs and LOD reduction;
3. read-only Unreal mesh/material inspection with unchanged tracked hashes;
4. packaged 44° opening council frame showing the whole speaker proxy and physical decision plane;
5. packaged command-weight front/back/contact views without a false rim intersection;
6. packaged Broken Crossing frame with all six signal silhouettes and unchanged interaction;
7. visible input-driven campaign progression and engagement return;
8. scene draw/GPU/game/memory evidence separated from Xvfb/noVNC presentation cost;
9. explicit human art/cinematic and historical-material review before final or marketing status.

The broad rain, road obstruction and mud pressure are grounded in the Chapter I source/claim register. The exact platform, materials, dimensions, joins and every playable arrangement remain project-authored reconstruction/interface design.

## Accepted blockout receipt

The deterministic Blender source and clean GLB/FBX imports pass exact bounds, topology, UV, material, LOD and collision checks. Unreal 5.8.1 reports `620 / 60` triangles, two UV channels on both LODs, `1.0 / 0.12` screen sizes, two exact material slots, one source convex hull, `128` lightmap resolution, lightmap UV1 and Nanite off. Runtime disables the admitted hull.

Both engine materials are ten-node opaque one-sided PBR graphs with one bounded two-octave Fast Gradient 3D variation node, five explicit outputs, zero textures, zero compile errors and no normal-input pretence. The pale-plaster first lookdev pass was rejected; the accepted packaged pass reads as dark firelit campaign earth with a narrow worked-wood perimeter.

The official editor target compiles and all `13 / 13` native SHI suites pass. A fresh Linux package advances the campaign from Act I to Act III, opens Broken Crossing, advances one pulse with all six live 3D states changing, then returns to the byte-guarded campaign without changing its turn, resources or decisions. Full hashes, screenshots and the deliberately bounded decision are recorded in `assets/provenance/shi-command-surface-v1.json` and `docs/production/evidence/unreal-command-surface-*.json`.

This closed the default-white command-surface defect only. The following wet-field checkpoint has since replaced the surrounding white engine plane; primitive figures/signals, interaction hands, shelter/vegetation, formations, weather, final lighting and human review remain red.
