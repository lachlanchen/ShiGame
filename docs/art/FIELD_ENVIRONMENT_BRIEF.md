# SHI wet-field environment brief

Status: production blockout contract · Daze Village rain-pressure setting · not a surveyed late-Qin site or reconstruction.

## Production problem

The accepted command-ground platform still sits inside a `24 × 24 m` default-white engine plane and a black void. The plane erases weather, scale and consequence, makes the figures float in a studio, and competes with the dark material hierarchy. This gate replaces that single visible defect without pretending that an invented command-stage layout is archaeological evidence.

## Narrative and spatial purpose

The environment must communicate that this council is assembled beside a waterlogged route under delayed-march pressure:

1. the command ground is a temporary dry-enough island, not a palace dais;
2. a compacted approach and paired ruts show repeated movement without presenting modern tire tracks;
3. shallow standing water and drainage lows make rain operationally legible;
4. the field falls into darkness and fog rather than ending as a white square;
5. every accepted camera, pointer ray, marker, command weight and engagement signal remains unchanged.

Rain, delayed movement and road pressure are grounded by the Chapter I source/claim register. The exact terrain relief, dimensions, puddles and route placement are project-authored environment design.

## Geometry contract

- Identity-root static environment centered on the command space, exactly `24 × 24 m` in X/Y.
- Central clearance remains below the command surface: terrain and water may not rise above `Z = -6 cm`; the platform bottom is `Z = -2 cm` and its marker plane is `Z = 14 cm`.
- A low-frequency field surface, bounded drainage fall and one compacted route replace the plane. No mountain diorama, strategic map, board-game grid or large heroic landmark.
- Paired depressions read as cart/foot traffic at environment scale, not automotive tire tread. No repeating high-frequency grooves.
- Shallow water is separate, restrained geometry outside the platform footprint. It is noninteractive and never claims physical simulation authority.
- One source convex receipt may describe the outer environment volume, but runtime collision, overlap events and navigation influence remain disabled.
- LOD0 target under 12,000 triangles; LOD1 below 4,000. UV0 and non-overlapping lightmap UV1 are required on each engine LOD. Nanite remains off at this scale unless measurement changes the decision.

## Material contract

### Wet field ground

- Dark umber/clay with low-frequency damp variation; not white, orange desert, polished rock or noisy procedural camouflage.
- Nonmetallic and broadly rough. No normal input until an authored/validated source proves scale and relighting.

- A smooth source vertex-color mask blends slightly cooler/darker trampled mud into the same continuous earth material where water compacts the route.
- Route distinction must come from restrained material and relief, not painted lines, a floating ribbon or a hard per-polygon boundary.

### Shallow rainwater

- Near-black brown-green reflection with high but not mirror-like smoothness.
- No tropical blue, foam, ocean normal, deep-water claim or transparent hole into the void.

## Runtime contract

- Exact asset identity, transform, bounds, two material slots, route vertex color, central clearance, noninteraction, no collision/navigation and council/engagement visibility fail closed before spawn.
- The environment is presentation only. It cannot mutate campaign or engagement authority, receive player clicks, hide during Broken Crossing, or change save data.
- A development-only review camera may inspect the whole terrain; it is excluded from normal builds and cannot substitute for the accepted dialogue lens.
- The default engine Plane is removed from `CreateCommandSpace`; engine-native primitives remain temporarily for figures/signals only.

## Rejection list

Reject a pass that introduces a bright perimeter, obvious square edge in normal framing, tiled noise, modern tire language, implausible mirror puddles, steep terrain that reads as a miniature landscape, water intersecting the command platform, false archaeological claims, fabricated writing, or a beauty render that fails the packaged council and engagement paths.

## Acceptance path

1. deterministic Blender source, oblique and profile inspection;
2. clean GLB/FBX round trip with topology, UV, material, bounds and LOD receipts;
3. isolated Unreal import/material inspection with tracked hashes unchanged by read-only runs;
4. native admission and negative tests;
5. fresh package and headless smoke;
6. visible normal council, whole-environment review and Broken Crossing progression/return;
7. physical-display performance repeated after later shelter, characters and formations;
8. human environment, historical-material, accessibility and cinematic review before final status.

This pass closes the default-white surrounding ground only. Shelter architecture, production characters/hands, vegetation, formation silhouettes, rain VFX, sky treatment and final lighting remain separate gates.
