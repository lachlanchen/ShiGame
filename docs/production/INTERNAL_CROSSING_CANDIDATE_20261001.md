# Consequential crossing: built internal chapter candidate

October 1, 2026. The revised crossing can now run as a production-compiled,
private web candidate rather than requiring the development server. This is a
playable checkpoint toward the opening → crossing → Chen sequence, not a public
rollout, mobile upload or film-quality claim.

## What the player can complete

The opening promise leads into three actual field orders. Their outcome changes
the campaign costs and the affected character's promise judgment. The result
survives pause/reload, appears in the chronicle, and carries into arrival at Chen.
Three council decisions then lead to the existing Fan Yang guarantee episode
and its authored conclusion. History supplies circumstances; the fictional ford,
households, personal dialogue, commands and numerical effects remain labeled
dramatization. No new history or rules were authored in this build checkpoint.

The supplied Tongjian volume 7 passage was re-read at the Daze rain/revolt,
arrival at Chen and Zhang Er/Chen Yu counsel sequence (private extracted lines
2655–2675). It grounds the historical order and political disagreement, not the
invented crossing encounter. Existing Qin-law uncertainty is not waived. Private
book text and modern annotations were not copied into the candidate repository.

## Distribution and save boundaries

- A separate entry and `internal-crossing` build mode select revision 2. Public
  URLs cannot enable that entry in the normal released bundle.
- Chapter, council and Fan Yang saves use `shi.internal.crossing-campaign.v2`
  and its suffixes. Released and earlier development slots are neither migrated
  nor overwritten. Corrupt internal saves are preserved instead of replaced by
  a fresh campaign or silently borrowed development state.
- The existing shared driver/rules/content are reused. No parallel narrative or
  copied resolver was introduced.
- Build output must be fresh and below ignored `.runtime`; the builder never
  writes `apps/web/dist`. Existing output, public output, native packaging and
  private-media flags are rejected. Private score/film/rain candidates and the
  later retreat/refuge draft remain excluded from both public and internal builds.
- CI now compiles/validates this candidate after the normal public build, but
  publishes only the existing normal web artifact. No internal artifact upload
  or store submission was added.

## Reproduce and next gate

After shared-workstation preflight, run `npm run build:internal-crossing`. It
validates, creates a fresh private output, builds and checks the channel and
payload boundaries, and prints its output directory. To test that exact output:

```bash
SHI_PLAYTEST_PRODUCTION=1 SHI_PLAYTEST_INTERNAL_CROSSING_DIR=<printed-output> \
  node scripts/playtest-retreat-visible.mjs crossing-v2
```

The controller uses the single SHI desktop and terminates its exact children
after evidence capture. Use `SHI_BROWSER_PROFILE` to reuse the established SHI
profile. To review manually, Vite preview accepts `--outDir <printed-output>/dist`
with localhost binding; no development flags or private media server is needed.
