# Foundation production roadmap

This roadmap is an ordered sequence of quality gates, not a one-year deadline and not permission for uncontrolled content generation. “Three years” means SHI may develop patiently without compromising the standard; it does not require release by a date. Work proceeds day by day, and an open gate remains open until its evidence passes.

Long-range continuation, film-level acceptance and release gates are defined in the active [film-level production charter](THREE_YEAR_FILM_LEVEL_PLAN.md). This roadmap is its synchronized detailed foundation-phase sequence; if scope changes, both documents must change in the same reviewed commit. Every task also passes the charter's story-and-film decision rubric: it must serve an authored beat, player agency or consequence, and a testable presentation need before assets or tools are commissioned.

## Foundation-phase outcomes

- A complete, replayable Daze-to-next-late-Qin vertical slice with a coherent authored ending and a shared-rules historical martial-command encounter. Chu–Han campaign scale belongs to later phases rather than this gate.
- A complete playable Web reference and stable Unreal cinematic desktop vertical-slice candidate; Unity remains a maintained shared-schema baseline. Public alpha is not a foundation-phase completion claim.
- Source, localization, asset, audio, build, accessibility, performance and playtest pipelines capable of supporting a full game.
- A small reviewed corpus and character/system bible that makes adding content safer rather than faster at any cost.
- A proven daily production rhythm in which open-source, online and AI tools remove bounded toil while story, performance, framing and player consequence remain authorial decisions.

## Operating cadence

- Daily: choose the highest-leverage story/player problem; finish one small durable increment; leave source, evidence and runtime state clean for the next day.
- Monday: player problem, evidence and scope review.
- Tuesday–Wednesday: one integrated gameplay increment.
- Thursday: story/film, historical/localization/asset review and automated validation.
- Friday: visible playtest, defect triage, progress log and clean build.
- Monthly: gate kill/continue decision based on player evidence, not elapsed time or sunk cost.

## Stop rules

- Do not add a chapter when the core loop is not producing meaningful disagreement between players.
- Do not scale AI asset generation faster than review capacity.
- Do not install or integrate a tool without a named story/film bottleneck, rights and security review, editable output, bounded trial and removal plan; prefer reuse when it demonstrably saves toil.
- Do not promise every named classical work will appear in play; use only relevant, rights-cleared evidence.
- Do not maintain Unity and Unreal gameplay forks.
- Do not call a gate complete when its build or review evidence is blocked, regardless of time spent.
