# One-year production roadmap

This roadmap is a sequence of quality gates, not permission for uncontrolled content generation. Dates are planning ranges beginning August 2026 and will change as evidence arrives.

## Year-one outcomes

- A complete, replayable late-Qin-to-Chu–Han campaign slice with a coherent authored ending and a shared-rules historical martial-command encounter.
- Shippable web release and stable Unreal cinematic desktop vertical slice; Unity remains a maintained shared-schema baseline.
- Source, localization, asset, audio, build, accessibility, performance and playtest pipelines capable of supporting a full game.
- A small reviewed corpus and character/system bible that makes adding content safer rather than faster at any cost.

## Weekly operating cadence

- Monday: player problem, evidence and scope review.
- Tuesday–Wednesday: one integrated gameplay increment.
- Thursday: historical/localization/asset review and automated validation.
- Friday: visible playtest, defect triage, progress log and clean build.
- Monthly: milestone kill/continue decision based on player evidence, not sunk cost.

## Stop rules

- Do not add a chapter when the core loop is not producing meaningful disagreement between players.
- Do not scale AI asset generation faster than review capacity.
- Do not promise every named classical work will appear in play; use only relevant, rights-cleared evidence.
- Do not maintain Unity and Unreal gameplay forks.
- Do not call a milestone complete when its build or review gate is blocked.
