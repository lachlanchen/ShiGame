# Part 01 — 雨夜中的承诺

2026-09-27 · A bounded production packet, not a finished movie or new store build.

The owner asks for slow, steady, elegant development: one coherent playable
part at a time. Review depth takes priority over output volume. Artistic quality
cannot be guaranteed by a model or a score; repeatable checks and rejection of
unsatisfactory takes are the commitment.

## One scene, one unresolved choice

The actual local-book reading and game-theory mapping are recorded in

Keep the existing fourteen-second, 30 fps, 421-sample ownership-exchange contract
for the controllable performance. Generated reference shots are not substitutes
for its frame-specific hand/contact checks. No stock triumph, retrospective
prophecy or narration explaining what the player ought to learn.

## Musia: refine the existing audition before generating more

Six original 45-second ACE-Step XL Turbo candidates already exist privately.
Their original WAV hashes were checked again, then the installed Musia APEX/MERT
tool scored all six serially on GPU 0, offline, with no new model downloads.
The standard `musia` environment worked. All six reports contain finite values;
a cuDNN plan warning occurred but inference completed successfully.

The five aesthetic dimensions were averaged only to suggest listening order;
popularity predictions were not used. The order is B, A, F, E, C, D, but means
span only approximately 2.69–2.76 on this model's 1–5 scale. This is not a
validated quality threshold, significant preference or reason to reject a track.
No candidate is selected. Full human listening and scene-fit review remain open.

For the shortlist, record exact timestamps of worthwhile motifs and problems:
unintended voice, fatigue, heroic overstatement, abrupt phrasing, weak silence,
unwanted similarity, or an ending that dictates the player's emotion. Listening
copies remain gain-matched; masters and seeds remain unchanged. The private
audition page allows notes to be exported without uploading anything.

After a motif is accepted, keep its actual musical material as the reference
for later tension and aftermath variations. Repeating the same text prompt or
seed does not prove thematic continuity. Separately generated tracks are not
phase-aligned stems. A loop needs its own boundary and listening checks.

playability review should the team make the next branch's consequences. Carry
forward the accepted cast/wardrobe versions, exact media hashes, register state,
weather/light state, music motif and canonical campaign revision.

action is one choice/consequence playback handoff with save-safe fallback.

open asset gates, not mass-generation tasks. No new image, video or

and its ongoing reviews unchanged; a future media-enabled beta needs its own
version, device checks, privacy/rights review and artifact record.
