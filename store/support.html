SHI · The Shape of Power

Help and support · LazyingArt LLC

SHI is an offline historical strategy narrative about late Qin China. This edition contains playable Chapter I, branching decisions, resource consequences, replayable field conditions, and a source ledger distinguishing historical claims from dramatization. Later chapters and the separate Unreal desktop combat scene are not included.

Playing

Read the position and field conditions. Select an order to inspect its intention and consequences, then confirm it to commit. Your choices change grain, trust, momentum, people and exposure. Continue resumes the locally saved chronicle. New chronicle starts a fresh seeded playthrough after confirmation.

Accessibility and language

Use the language and reduced-motion controls. iOS supports native text sizing and VoiceOver. Interface labels support eleven languages; historical narrative currently uses English and Simplified Chinese, with English fallback for untranslated passages. No network connection or account is required for play.

Saved progress

Progress is stored on the device, not synchronized between platforms. Do not uninstall to troubleshoot a save problem. If a save cannot be opened, contact support first. If saving fails because storage is full, free space before continuing.
