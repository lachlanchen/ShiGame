SHI · Privacy policy

Effective September 26, 2026 · LazyingArt LLC

SHI: The Shape of Power is an offline historical strategy game. The iOS and Android app identifier is art.lazying.shi.

What stays on your device

Your decisions, campaign seed, selected language, motion preference and (where available) audio settings are stored locally. SHI has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, chat, cloud-save service, or in-app purchases. We do not collect or transmit gameplay or device data from the app to our servers.

Purchases and platform services

The app is a one-time paid download. Apple or Google handles the transaction under its own policies; we do not receive your payment-card information. Operating-system backups and optional store diagnostics or TestFlight feedback follow your device and platform settings. iOS device backups may include game progress; the Android app disables app-data backup.

Optional external links and support

The source ledger can open historical reference websites in your browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies. If you choose to email support, we receive the information you send and use it to respond and resolve your request. Do not send passwords or sensitive personal information.

Deleting data

A new chronicle replaces the active playthrough after confirmation. Deleting the app removes its local storage; device backups, if any, are managed through your operating system. An unreadable iOS save may be preserved locally for recovery when you explicitly start over. There is no server account to delete. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to necessary legal retention.
