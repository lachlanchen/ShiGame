# Jinyang walkable world v1

Original scene geometry authored for SHI with `scripts/build-jinyang-world.py` and Blender 4.0.2, then imported with `scripts/import-jinyang-world-unreal.py` into Unreal 5.8.1. No downloaded mesh, model weights, third-party texture or private book is embedded. Materials are original procedural color/noise/water-normal graphs. Rights: project-owned original work under the repository's rights-reserved policy; company handles external distribution licensing.

The editable `.blend`, six FBX layers and their manifest are source assets. Unreal `.uasset` counterparts live in `/Game/SHI/Art/JinyangWorld`. World coordinates are authored in centimetres, expressed in Blender metres; the importer converts units. Runtime scenery scale `(1,-1,1)` compensates the FBX handedness conversion so the walkable routes align with the unchanged campaign sites. Tunic/robe meshes are symmetric normalized figure parts, not full character scans or archaeological dress reproductions.

Historical basis: the Jinyang siege recounted retrospectively in *Zizhi Tongjian*, volume I; supplied original and modern translation consulted privately. Flooded homes, refuge and the besiegers' coalition motivate the scene. Buildings, landscaping, distances, costumes and resident routines are game reconstruction, not a surveyed historical city or documented performance. No private translation is redistributed.

Intended use: local explorable desktop development chapter. Four scenery layers total 60,055 triangles; two clothing parts add 424 triangles per complete pair. Ground/Architecture provide static walking collision. Details/Horizon and water have no gameplay collision. Exploration and resident animation never modify orders or resource totals.

Review: two in-engine visual passes and actual keyboard walking inspected on 2026-10-03. The first faceted horizon, clipped introduction and static-lighting warning were rejected and corrected. Street, stairs and northern levee were walked; landmark panels were read in-engine. Accepted as an integrated **development environment**, not final high-quality environment/character art, exact historical architecture, a mobile-qualified asset or a claim of human art approval. Current figures and foliage still need substantial art direction and replacement/refinement. See the associated production receipt for package/input/performance checks.
